# Sugid

Static literary map of Negros Occidental. Open `index.html` in a browser to explore the five featured city books and the additional “Works of Authors From Other Cities/Municipalities in Negros Occidental” collection.

The city archive, sourcebook notes, and additional writers collection are organized in `content/`. Source PDFs are kept in `sources/`. The additional collection reproduces the two public-domain Adelina Gurrea poems included in its source compilation and links to external sources for other works.
